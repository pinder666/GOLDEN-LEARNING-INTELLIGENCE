## The three generic columns and who they really are

### Column 1 — "The group chat" = WhatsApp (and team text threads)
- What most grassroots coaches ACTUALLY run the team on. The real competitor.
- Does: messaging (chaotic, unthreaded, no read-tracking of who's coming Saturday).
- Does not: anything else. RSVPs are "who saw my message," dues are chased in public, photos of kids sit in a chat owned by nobody.
- Cost: nothing on paper. The real price is the coach's evenings — our cost-row line.

### Column 2 — "The team admin app" = TeamSnap, Spond, Heja (+ MOJO, SportsEngine/season side, Stack Team App, TeamLinkt)
| Platform | Price | Payment cut | Ads | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TeamSnap | $10.83–13.75/mo annual; Premium $18.99/mo | ~3.25% + $1.50 per family transaction | Yes on free tier | Free tier: 15-player cap, NO RSVP tracking, no drills. **Premium/TeamSnap+ DOES include drills + practice plans** (MLS, Jr. NBA, NFL Flag packs) → our chart grants the grey ✓ on the drill row because of this. |
| Spond | Core features free | ~$1.00 + 3.29% per US transaction | No | Cleanest free offer; monetizes entirely through the payment cut + club products. |
| Heja | Free tier; paid from ~$8.33/mo | ~2–5% on parent payments | Yes on free tier | Swedish origin, youth-focused, clean UX. |
| MOJO Sports | Free for coaches; ~$59.99/yr premium | — | — | **The sleeper — but smaller than it talks.** Jason's own check (2026-10-01): advertises "5 million families" yet shows ~100k app downloads — marketing number vs real footprint. Free practice plans powered by NBA/MLB/NFL Flag + FC Barcelona content, scheduling, chat, attendance. Best free drills offer in the category. Multi-sport, US-centric. |
- None of them: voice-to-polished-message, per-family language delivery, development records, child badges, tactics board, video analysis, any mental layer. All multi-sport by design.
- Universal truth used in our cost row: **every major one takes a cut of family payments.** Ads or monthly fees vary by tier; the cut is constant.

### Column 3 — "The video platform" = Veo, Hudl (+ Trace, Pixellot)
- Veo: Cam 3 ~$1,533 / 5G ~$1,886 hardware + subscriptions $42–91/mo. Realistic first year for a club: ~$2,600–3,000.
- Hudl: Focus Flex ~$1,999 hardware + Bronze $400 / Silver $1,000 / Gold $1,600 per team per year. Pricing famously opaque.
- They genuinely do AI video analysis (our chart's grey ✓) — recording, highlights, tagging, telestration on higher tiers.
- They do not: team admin, parent messaging, development records, drills, a creatable/animatable tactics board, anything mental, anything priced for one grassroots coach.

## Adjacent threats NOT in the chart (know them for conversations)

- **TeamOrg / Arly Teams** — tiny admin apps that DO offer AI message translation (Arly: Spanish + Simplified Chinese; TeamOrg Pro: AI translation + "message polish"). Nobody owns them at scale, and neither does voice→AI→approved-message. If a prospect names them: acknowledge, then pivot to the whole right-hand column — translation is one row of fourteen.
- **TopTekkers** (ages 5–13) — home TECHNICAL training: skill videos, points, trophies, coach/parent portals, coach can assign. Trusted by EPL/MLS academies. Closest thing to our home loop, but it trains feet, not the mind, and has no coach cockpit, no team admin, no messaging.
- **Techne Futbol** (Yael Averbuch) — weekly technical workouts, streaks, leaderboards, "Training Socks" rewards. Same shape as TopTekkers: individual, technical, no team layer.
- **Mental/cognitive training**: Be Your Best (VR scanning trainer, +28% scan frequency in 9 weeks — needs a VR headset), NeuroTracker (3D object tracking), IntelliGym (computer-based, 2×30min/week), Train Effective ("Game Brain"). **None is coach-connected, team-wide, or in the parent's phone as a daily habit.** The "mental performance program in every player's home, prescribed through the coach's cockpit" combination remains unoccupied. This is the moat — and 2026 trend pieces say cognitive training is the year's breakout category, which means the window is now.
- **PlayMetrics (+ SportsEngine, now merged)** — the "club operating system": registration, tryouts, scheduling, AND a real player-development module (curriculum, evaluations, drill library, evals stored on player profiles). Sold to CLUB DIRECTORS at club pricing, not to a coach. **And it is YOUTH SPORTS broadly, not soccer** (Jason 2026-10-01: "we only focus on soccer, we don't care about baseball, basketball") — the soccer-only line beats it in every academy room. **This is the enemy in White Label / academy conversations**, not in coach conversations. Our answers: per-coach price vs club contract; development the child and family actually see vs evaluation forms in an office; the mental layer they don't have; Spanish-native.

## Chart cell-by-cell defense (as shipped)

- Schedule/RSVP, messaging, roster, dues, drills → admin app grey ✓ (TeamSnap paid tiers cover all five; free tier covers less — we grant the category its best self, which makes the chart unattackable).
- Group chat → messaging ✓ only.
- Video platform → AI video analysis ✓ only.
- Blank everywhere else, verified: no admin app or video platform does voice→polished message, per-family language (at scale — see TeamOrg/Arly above), development records (at coach level — PlayMetrics is club-tier), child badges (TopTekkers does, but it is not in any of the three categories a coach owns), creatable tactics board, mental program at home, or soccer-only focus.
- Cost row: all figures above.

## Objection one-liners (for DMs and calls, where names ARE allowed)

- "I use TeamSnap." — "Keep it if you love admin. TeamSnap runs your paperwork; it has never made one of your players better. And it takes 3.25% + $1.50 every time a family pays you."
- "MOJO is free." — "MOJO gives you NBA practice plans for a multi-sport app. We give you a soccer-only cockpit where the drills land in the child's home, in their daily training, in two languages."
- "We have a Veo." — "Perfect, keep filming. Veo shows you the game. We develop the player between games, and our analyst talks you through the play instead of handing you a timeline."
- "What about Be Your Best for the mental side?" — "VR headset per child, no coach connection. Ours lives in the parent's phone, 20 minutes a day, and your drills ride along."
- "PlayMetrics does development." — "For the club office, at club prices. Ours lives with the coach and the family, and the mental layer doesn't exist there at all."
