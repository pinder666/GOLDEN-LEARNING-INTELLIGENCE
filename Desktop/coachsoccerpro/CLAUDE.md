# CoachSoccerPro

A mentorship platform disguised as a youth soccer app. The soccer is the hook. The real product is confidence, habit formation, and making every child feel seen.

## What This Actually Is

**For coaches:** An amplification system. Takes a coach who cares and gives them tools to have 10x impact — voice-to-AI messaging, one-tap encouragement, development tracking, professional presence with parents.

**For parents:** A window into their child's growth. Not game scores — real development. Badges, streaks, coach notes, progress.

**For children:** A pride machine. Their living player card. Badges they earned. Leaderboard where they ALWAYS appear (no one invisible). Pro habits from Messi and Ronaldo. A journey they own.

## Core Belief

The coach is the most trusted adult outside of family in that child's life. Most coaches WANT to be mentors — they just don't have tools or time. We give them both.

## Stack

## Spanish Is Core

Spanish-speaking market is primary, not secondary. Every string, every badge name, every pro habit, every coach template — written in both English and Spanish from day one. Not translated after. Written natively.

## The Child Experience (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Every child on the leaderboard** — no top 5 only. If you're #15 of 15, you're still ON the board. You exist.
2. **Badges are the emotional core** — collectible, earnable, visible. Multiple paths to earn (effort, not just talent).
3. **Pro habits from real players** — "Messi sleeps 9 hours." Kids copy heroes.
4. **Streak system** — attendance flames, effort streaks, streak shields so one miss doesn't destroy progress.
5. **Card evolution** — Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond frames. Earned, not bought.

## Rules — Never Break These

- Never show "Top 5 only" — every child appears on every leaderboard
- Never send nutrition/mentorship from "the app" — always from Coach
- Never use placeholder data — empty states are honest states
- Never build features halfway — complete or don't start
- Never hardcode English — every string goes through i18n
- Spanish copy is written natively, not translated from English
- One screen, one feature, one fix at a time
- Show schema before touching database — wait for confirmation
- Run and verify after each build step
- Pro habits use real quotes from real players with real sources
