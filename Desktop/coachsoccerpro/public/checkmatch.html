CHECKMATCH — THE LAWS

The objective is not to shoot. It is to make the save impossible. The keeper is the last wall: he covers his own square and every square touching it. A shot into covered space is always saved. You score only by removing his protection — dragging defenders away, pinning him to one post, and striking the square he can no longer reach. The goal is proof that the position was already won.

The pieces — 7 a side

GK — the keeper. Moves 1 square, may never leave his box (the 4 central columns of his own two rows). Covers his square + all touching squares against shots.

DF ×2 — defenders. Straight lines only, up to 2 squares.

WG ×2 — wingers. Diagonals only, up to 3 squares. They own the angles.

PM — the playmaker. Any direction, up to 2 squares.

ST — the striker. Jumps in an L: two squares, then one to the side — arriving where nobody tracked the run. With the ball he moves 1 square only: close control.

The turn — three phases

1 SCAN. Before you may act, tap the opponent piece you believe acts next. Right or wrong, it is logged — your Scan Rating is your awareness score across the game.

2 ACT. One action: move a piece, dribble the ball, pass, shoot, or tackle.

3 READ (optional). Predict your opponent's exact next action: their piece and its destination square. If you are right, you earn TEMPO — two actions in a future turn. Reading habits is how you gain speed.

The ball

A pass travels in a straight or diagonal ray and stops at the first player it meets — teammate receives, opponent intercepts. You cannot pass into empty space. Structure comes before the pass.

Dribbling carries the ball but slows you: movement range −1. The striker with the ball moves 1 square only.

The Pressure Law

A tackle is only legal when your players outnumber theirs in the squares around the carrier. One player never wins the ball alone. The press is collective or it is nothing. A tackled player is displaced toward his own goal and BEATEN for one turn. No piece ever leaves the board.

Shooting

A shot is a pass aimed at the opponent's goal mouth (the two marked squares), released from inside their final three rows, with a clear lane. If the keeper stands on or beside the target square — always saved, and he keeps the ball. Strip his cover first. Then strike.

Territory

The first time your carrier stands in each row of the opponent's half, you claim it: +1 territory. If no goal falls in 60 turns, territory decides the match. Build-up is never wasted.

Winning

One goal ends the game. Everything before it is the argument; the goal is the conclusion.
