# CoachSoccerPro Research Report: Coach, Club & Academy Pain Points

**Report Date:** September 26, 2026  
**Purpose:** Data-driven landing page copy foundation

## Executive Summary

The youth soccer market is at a breaking point. **270 MILLION people play football worldwide** — 4% of the global population. Participation is at an all-time high, but the volunteer infrastructure is collapsing. Coaches are burning out. Kids are quitting. Clubs are bleeding money through payment failures. **The system needs tools that actually help coaches coach — not more admin burden.**

## THE DATA (With Sources)

### 1. THE COACH CRISIS

| Statistic | Data | Source |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Admin burden | **16-20 hours/week** on administrative work that has nothing to do with the field | [Project Play / Aspen Institute](https://projectplay.org/state-of-play-2024-coaching-trends) |
| First-year quit rate | **40% of volunteer coaches quit within year 1** because workload is unsustainable | [Project Play](https://projectplay.org/state-of-play-2024-coaching-trends) |
| Volunteer shortage | **82% of youth league leaders** have difficulty finding volunteers | [National Recreation and Park Association, Oct 2024](https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2025/10/27/youth-coaching-shortage-) |
| Coach shortage perception | **80% of parents** believe there's a shortage of high-caliber coaches | [Aspen Institute National Coach Survey](https://youthsportsbusinessreport.com/aspen-institute-state-of-play-youth-sports-coaching-trends-in-2024-evolution-and-challenges/) |
| Referee exodus | **50,000 officials stopped officiating** since 2018-19 | [Project Play](https://projectplay.org/state-of-play-2024-coaching-trends) |

**THE PROBLEM CSP SOLVES:** Coaches spend 16-20 hours per week on admin. CSP cuts that to minutes. Voice-to-AI messaging, one-tap attendance, automated payment tracking.

### 2. THE PARENT COMMUNICATION NIGHTMARE

| Statistic | Data | Source |
|-----------|------|--------|
| #1 burnout cause | **Parent conflict, toxic sidelines, late-night complaints** are primary stressors | [PLOS One Research](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0333559) |
| Officials' complaint | **Over 40% of officials** cite unruly parents as biggest job impediment | [State of Play 2024](https://projectplay.org/state-of-play-2024-coaching-trends) |
| Communication chaos | Coaches juggle **WhatsApp, email, text, Facebook** — fragmented, overwhelming | [Jersey Watch](https://www.jerseywatch.com/blog/best-app-for-coaches-to-communicate-with-parents) |
| Playing time pressure | Parents demand justification for **playing time, training decisions, team selection** | [Frontiers Psychology](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1770500/full) |

**THE PROBLEM CSP SOLVES:** One professional communication channel. Voice note → AI polish → professional message. Parent sees coach as organized authority, not someone to argue with. Parent dashboard keeps them informed without coach chasing.

### 3. THE PAYMENT BLACK HOLE

| Statistic | Data | Source |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Online vs. Offline collection | **98.2% collection online** vs 83.6% offline = **15 point difference** | [Centro / Youth Soccer Finance Guide](https://withcentro.com/blog/youth-soccer-club-finances-guide) |
| Payment plan failure | Payment #1: 0.6% fail → Payment #9: **4.5% fail** (7-8x escalation) | [Signature Locker](https://signaturelocker.com/blogs/program-directors-playbook/that-payment-plan-you-offered-is-great-for-families-it-might-be-killing-your-cash-flow) |
| Real dollar impact | If $30K expected, 15% delinquency = **$4,500 short** | [Signature Locker](https://signaturelocker.com/blogs/program-directors-playbook/that-payment-plan-you-offered-is-great-for-families-it-might-be-killing-your-cash-flow) |
| Registration-to-payment gap | Most common failure: registration submitted but **payment never collected** | [The Futures App](https://www.thefuturesapp.com/news/youth-sports-registration-software-what-clubs-actually-need) |

**THE PROBLEM CSP SOLVES:** Stripe Connect built-in. Automated payment requests. Automated reminders. Dashboard shows who's paid, who hasn't. Coach doesn't chase money.

### 4. THE PLAYER DROPOUT EPIDEMIC

| Statistic | Data | Source |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Dropout rate | **70% of kids quit sports by age 13** | [Forbes, NBC26](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sarahhernholm/2026/06/22/the-375-billion-youth-sports-market-has-a-retention-problem/) |
| Average quit age | **12.93 years old** (Aspen Institute 2026 survey of 4,000 youth) | [Aspen Institute](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sarahhernholm/2026/06/22/the-375-billion-youth-sports-market-has-a-retention-problem/) |
| #1 reason for quitting | **Lack of enjoyment** — not injuries, not cost, not time | [Systematic review of 43 studies](https://joinstriveon.com/blog/why-young-athletes-quit) |
| Bad coaching impact | **21-23% of players who quit** name bad coaching as top complaint | [StrivOn Research](https://joinstriveon.com/blog/why-young-athletes-quit) |
| Market size | **$37.5 BILLION** youth sports market | [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sarahhernholm/2026/06/22/the-375-billion-youth-sports-market-has-a-retention-problem/) |

## KEY MESSAGING TAKEAWAYS

1. **Don't sell features. Sell the problem solved.**
   - ❌ "We have a calendar feature"
   - ✅ "Stop the 11pm WhatsApp scramble about tomorrow's practice"

2. **Use the stats.** They're credible, specific, and alarming.

3. **Show, don't tell.** Screenshots of the actual dashboard are worth 10x more than stock photos.

4. **Spanish is core.** 16.8M US players. Huge Hispanic participation. EN/ES from day 1.

5. **The real product is the child's experience.** Coaches buy because it makes their kids light up.
