Sarah & James Whitmore
Table 7  ·  8:00pm  ·  Party of 2  ·  Anniversary Dinner
Who Is Coming & Why
Their seventh anniversary. James booked three weeks ago — longer than usual — which suggests this evening has been thought about. Sarah mentioned in her quiz that she values atmosphere over theatrics. They are not here for performance. They are here to be present with each other in a room that understands them.
What the Server Should Know
James tends to lead the conversation about wine but defers to Sarah on food. Do not assume he is the decision-maker at the table. Give them time. Their previous visit ran to three hours and they left satisfied — tonight should be allowed to breathe the same way. Sarah has a mild intolerance to shellfish. Nothing on the tasting menu is affected, but confirm quietly before the amuse-bouche arrives.
Sommelier Intelligence
James selected "old world, structured, not too fruit-forward" in his profile. Last visit he ordered the Barolo by the glass twice. Have the 2018 Giacomo Conterno Cascina Francia ready for a recommendation — not a suggestion, a recommendation, delivered with confidence. If they move to dessert, the Sauternes from bin 47 will close the evening perfectly.
What to Avoid
They are a quiet couple. Do not seat them near the service station or the large party on Table 12 tonight. Avoid over-checking. They will signal when they need attention. Previous feedback noted that they appreciated when the team "seemed to know without being asked." That is the standard for tonight.
The Unrequested Gesture
Sarah mentioned in her intake that their first date was at a restaurant that used to serve a burnt honey ice cream. She has never forgotten it. Chef should know: a small quenelle of smoked honey gelato presented between courses — no explanation, no theatre — will mean more to her than any dish on the menu tonight.
